The editor

Built for explaining code, not just prose

Headings & structure

Break an article into headings so readers can scan it, skim it, and still find the part they actually needed.

Code blocks & inline code

Drop in a full code block for a function or a snippet, or mark a single word as inline code without breaking your sentence.

Tables & quotes

Lay out a comparison in a table, or pull a line from documentation or another author into a proper quote.

Images

Add screenshots, diagrams or a cover image wherever the explanation needs a picture instead of another paragraph.

Distraction-light writing

The editor stays out of the way while you write, and gets out of the way again when you read it back.

Written in one place

Write, edit and format from the same screen. No separate tool to draft in and paste over from.

Drafts

Write at your own pace

Your draft saves itself automatically as you write, so a closed tab or a dead battery does not cost you a paragraph. Come back to it an hour later or a week later and pick up where you stopped.

A draft stays a draft until you decide it is ready. Nobody sees it, it does not appear in Explore or on a topic page, and it will not show up on your public profile until you publish it yourself.

Publishing

From draft to Explore, straight away

One button, and it is live

Publish and your article appears immediately on Explore and on the page for whichever of the sixteen topics it belongs to.

Ranked by real signals

Articles on Explore and topic pages are ordered by the views, likes and comments they earn, not by who posted first.

After you publish

Publishing is the start, not the end

Once an article is live, readers can like it, bookmark it for later, and leave comments underneath it. You get a notification for new comments, replies, likes and new followers, with an unread count so you never miss a response.

Your dashboard tracks views, likes, comments and followers for every article you have published, over time. Your public profile lists your published work alongside your followers and following, starting from your very first piece.

Changed your mind about something you published, or found a mistake. You can edit a published article at any time, move it back to draft, or delete it, all from your own dashboard.

Questions

Writing, answered

Do I need an account to write?

Yes. Sign up for free, then start a draft from your dashboard whenever an idea is ready to become an article.

Does it cost anything to publish?

No. Writing and publishing are free, and there is no paywall on your own words.

Will I lose my work if I close the tab by accident?

Your draft saves itself automatically as you write, so closing the tab mid-sentence should not cost you the paragraph.

Which topic should I publish under?

Pick whichever of the sixteen live topics fits your article best, from JavaScript to Cybersecurity. You can browse them all on the Topics page.

Can I take an article down later?

Yes. Move a published article back to draft or delete it entirely, any time, from your dashboard.
